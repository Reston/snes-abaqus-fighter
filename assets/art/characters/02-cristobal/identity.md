# Cristóbal Mackenzie

Cristóbal is the agile rushdown silhouette in the character-specific art pass.
The orange rolled-sleeve work jacket gives him a bright warm read against the
navy trousers and deep blue arcade outlines. His stance leans forward, his
guard is lively, and the animation shapes keep the hands and feet large enough
to read after reduction to SNES sprite size.

The face and hair are cartoon design cues derived from
`assets/references/fighter-02.jpg`: an adult slightly long oval face, high
forehead with a slightly receding natural hairline, short cropped **jet-black**
hair, a fuller rounded **black** beard and moustache, natural dark brows,
prominent ears, straight nose, and calm confident eyes. The user-specified
black hair and beard color is authoritative; neither may read brown, auburn, or
orange. The atlas is original game art and depicts a fictional fighter, not a
realistic employee portrait or an assigned real-world role.

Readability targets:

- orange/rust jacket ramp with a pale shirt opening;
- charcoal fitted trousers and dark shoes;
- deep navy contour and approximately fifteen usable material colors;
- right-facing standing poses with a consistent feet baseline;
- clear separation between all sixteen cells and genuinely transparent gaps;
- enlarged fists, face, and shoes for low-resolution play.

Pose order is row-major: idle guard, front walk, back walk, low crouch guard;
tucked jump, extended jab, cross punch, high side kick; low sweep, raised
block, hurt recoil, victory; two-hand energy cast with no projectile, rising
uppercut, horizontal KO with head to the left, forward shoulder lunge.
