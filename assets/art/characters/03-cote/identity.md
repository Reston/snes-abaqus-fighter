# José Ignacio “Cote” Villarroel

Cote is the sturdy close-range silhouette in the character-specific art pass.
His upright posture, squared fists, and broad shoulder line give him a firm
arcade read. The crimson waistcoat supplies a strong warm focal color while
the rolled white sleeves, dark trousers, and navy contours keep his body parts
separate after reduction to SNES sprite size.

The face and hair are cartoon design cues derived from
`assets/references/fighter-03.jpg`: short naturally swept copper/ginger hair,
a full rounded ginger beard and moustache, paler skin, a long oval face, and a
high forehead. His eyes stay normal and his mouth stays calm and confident.
The atlas is original game art and depicts a fictional fighter, not a realistic
employee portrait or an assigned real-world role.

## Likeness revision

The v2 likeness pass uses `assets/references/fighter-03.jpg` only as a broad
identity reference. It keeps the original fictional cartoon fighter, crimson
waistcoat, pale sleeves, body, and all sixteen pose silhouettes while refining
the hair, beard, and head cues across every cell. The selected source was
generated with the built-in imagegen edit workflow and is recorded in
`likeness-v2.json`.

Readability targets:

- crimson/burgundy waistcoat ramp over a bright rolled white shirt;
- charcoal fitted trousers and dark shoes;
- deep navy contour and approximately fifteen usable material colors;
- right-facing standing poses with a consistent feet baseline;
- clear separation between all sixteen cells and genuinely transparent gaps;
- enlarged fists, face, and shoes for low-resolution play.

Pose order is row-major: idle guard, front walk, back walk, low crouch guard;
tucked jump, extended jab, cross punch, high side kick; low sweep, raised
block, hurt recoil, victory; two-hand energy cast with no projectile, rising
uppercut, horizontal KO with head to the left, forward shoulder lunge.
