# Ivo Kovacevic

This is the game-fiction identity sheet for roster slot 01. The source portrait at `assets/references/fighter-01.jpg` is an appearance reference only; it is not shipped as game art. The fighter is rendered as an original cartoon sprite character.

## Appearance and outfit

- Short neatly swept dark brown hair and a calm, focused expression.
- Taller, medium-athletic, long-legged strategist silhouette with a narrower waist and precise guarded posture.
- Emerald tailored rolled-sleeve jacket over a dark charcoal button shirt, deep charcoal trousers, dark shoes, warm light-olive skin, and a deep green/navy contour.
- The emerald jacket, dark hair, long legs, and composed stance are the primary recognition cues at SNES fighting distance.

## Readability notes

The atlas uses a limited cel-shaded palette with a dark outline and two to four value steps per material. The emerald jacket keeps Ivo separate from Damian’s cobalt costume, while the charcoal shirt and trousers create a clear long-legged silhouette. The dark hair, precise hands, extended reach, and restrained expression are exaggerated enough to survive a roughly 64×80-pixel in-game reduction. There are no logos, lettering, weapons, effects, or background elements.
