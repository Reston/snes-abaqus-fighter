# Four-button combat and native presentation

The v0.21.0 candidate responds to the user's request for four attack buttons,
a grounded shadow and a cleaner health/round-win display.

## Controls

| Face button | P1 keyboard | Attack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Y | A | Light punch |
| X | S | Strong punch |
| B | Z | Light kick |
| A | X | Strong kick |

Down+X is the universal uppercut; toward+X at close range is a throw.
Down+A is a sweep. Down+Y is the quick special; a quarter-circle followed
by either punch also performs the character's special. In the air, A keeps
the short rear reach for a close cross-up. L/R no longer start or buffer a
combat attack. They retain their home and paused CPU Training level controls.
Both physical controller ports use the same layout. Pause/menu priorities,
relative-back guard and Training's ignored P2 gameplay input remain.

The internal action IDs and six-slot profile arrays remain stable to preserve
the native Fighter/Wave and animation contracts. The four neutral face-button
actions are 1/3/4/5; Down+A selects the existing sweep action 6. CPU input now
uses the same four face buttons and directional commands. Down+X takes
priority over a stale motion latch; the final forward+punch quarter-circle
input still selects the special.

## Floor contact and HUD

The shadow uses one shallow contact ellipse made from the existing two
mirrored 16x16 sprites per fighter. Its left half starts at centerX-16,
the right half at centerX, both at screen Y 200 (floor-1). Its four painted
rows touch the feet; jumping keeps the shadow on the floor. The OAM entries
remain slots 64/65 and 66/67, tile 430, with their original attributes. The
low waves end their 16-pixel fetch rectangle on row 198, above the shadows;
the all-stage overflow checks still cover crowded effects.

The five-row HUD has gold nameplates, capped gradient health bars, a framed
timer, thin energy gauges, closed diamond round-win medals and a gold bottom
rule. Health turns red at one quarter or less. The marks show round wins in
the existing best-of-three match. They are earned through normal gameplay.

Health and energy retain their original pixel formulas, tile IDs, mirrored
origins and caching. The HUD still transfers 320 bytes. The guide still uses
the existing 1,472-byte three-tick path. No extra OBJ slots, mutable state,
WRAM addresses, trace offsets, actor streams, palette banks or DMA ranges
are introduced. The accent bank is the existing BG2 palette 1, reset on
scene entry and replaced by a portrait's own palette in portrait scenes.

Only `ui_tiles.bin`, `ui_pal.bin` and `effects.bin` change among the 219
tracked generated assets. Fighter banks, portraits, stages, ambient cells,
menu plates and audio retain their v0.20.0 bytes. Damian's approved v5 art
and all other fighter identities are preserved.

## Verification and review

The [focused report](four-button-hud-report.json) drives all twelve fighters
on both ports, checks the four normal actions, ignored shoulders, uppercut,
sweep and floor-fixed shadow entries. A controller-only match earns a round
medal, then reduces the next round's opponent to low health for the
[native capture](screenshots/four-button-hud/earned-medal-low-health.png).

The six native suites and independent smoke record their exact cartridge
hashes. The [candidate note](RELEASE-v0.21.0.md) and
[verification record](four-button-hud-verification.json) summarize the final
results. Automated work is headless and captures audio without playback.
Human review of the new controls and presentation remains open.

The four-choice CPU changes some deterministic fight phases. Updated test
fixtures keep their actual-contact, wake, native-cadence and KO requirements:
the wake probe uses short uppercuts and recloses after recoil; a separately
measured Normal phase proves a free anti-air opportunity can be declined;
the KO pilot throws while approaching and finishes low-health opponents with
an uppercut, retaining the zero-HP airborne-flight proof. The raw controller
host remains unchanged. Historical cadence reproduction inputs are retained.

Full release-scale matrix, independent combat, live-hour, audio, fresh-export
and offline-package evidence is not regenerated by these focused checks.
The v0.19.0 archive remains the previous full offline checkpoint.
