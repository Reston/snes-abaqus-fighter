One combined review

  1. Read a heavy windup, block or evade, then punish. Compare light/strong/guard recoil at center, walls and after a trade.
  2. Watch support foot, hip, guard and striking limb together. Check visible fist/boot/projectile edges against hits and misses from both ports.
  3. Jump, use all four air buttons and land with each fighter. Try a rear airborne A cross-up and immediate movement after landing.
  4. Visit both sides of all three stages. Check feet, floor, connected scenery, impact shake and the paused guide.
  5. Use several full-meter supers. Judge charge/contact/recovery and try interrupting or blocking them.
