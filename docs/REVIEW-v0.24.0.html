What to judge together

  1. Read and punish: walk/retreat, compare light and heavy attacks, block a heavy and punish recovery. Try Y-hit → X and B-hit → A confirms.
  2. Contact and recoil: compare light, heavy and guard reactions at center and walls. Try tip-range misses, projectiles, trades and rear airborne A kicks on both ports.
  3. Air movement: jump, guard, attack and move immediately after landing with every fighter. Pay attention to Ivo, Negger, Fernando and Reston's adjusted heights.
  4. Presentation: visit both sides of all three stages, pause the guide, then trigger every fighter's super. Check coherent scenery, feet/shadows, likeness and readable phases.

Feedback should name the fighter pair, stage, player port, input and visible issue, plus the frozen ROM identity when available. Human responses are recorded separately in review feedback.
